A Universe in Expansion

The Shambhala Saga is not cold or distant science fiction. Here, space exploration exists alongside intense human bonds, utopian communities, impossible choices, and profound questions about who we are.

Beyond the Planet

Orbital stations, lunar colonies, and warp travel to unknown worlds. In Shambhala, technology stretches the limits of humanity, yet emotion remains its center—shaping every discovery, every connection, and every step into the unknown.

Science with a Human Pulse

Physics, biology, engineering, philosophy, and artificial intelligence are not merely part of the setting; they are woven into the characters’ choices, relationships, and ways of understanding the universe.

Imagined Communities

Shambhala imagines spaces where women create new forms of community shaped by knowledge, care, solidarity, and resistance.

Love, Loss, and Transformation

Each book expands the universe while drawing us deeper into the inner lives of its characters: their desires, their connections, and the discoveries that transform them from within.

A Different Presence

Annie is not a conventional character. Her presence reshapes the boundaries between companionship, memory, and connection.

Narrative Constellation

The Four Books of the Saga

Each book opens onto a different phase of the journey: discovery, resistance, expansion, and horizon. Together, they form an emotional and cosmic arc that widens with every step.

Book 1

Callisto 2.0

Discovery and Belonging

Shambhala Space Station, 2097. Solitary physicist Callisto Collins receives an unexpected invitation to join Portal al Porvenir, a foundation at the forefront of visionary scientific research. What at first seems like the professional opportunity of a lifetime soon becomes something far deeper: a women-led scientific community where brilliance, cooperation, and human warmth offer her something she has never truly known—a sense of belonging.

As her work on warp technology moves closer to a breakthrough, Callisto is drawn ever deeper into the Foundation’s world—its ideals, its mysteries, and the relationships that begin to reshape her life, revealing a future she never thought possible.

Book 2

Cupid’s Arrow

Absence, Resistance, and Resolve

As Shambhala ventures into the unknown, Pavani remains in Arcadia, her heart suspended between Callisto’s absence and the threat gathering over the world she has come to call home. What begins as a painful farewell soon becomes a full-scale crisis: the Moon comes under military pressure, the community is watched and tested, and the women left behind must learn to resist with intelligence, dignity, and strength.

As fear spreads and the deeper forces behind the crisis begin to emerge, Pavani is changed as well. In the midst of uncertainty, she finds a steadier voice, a deeper courage, and the resolve to step fully into her own place within the struggle unfolding around her.

Emotional core: distance, resistance, strength, and resolve.

Narrative scale: a more intimate perspective that deepens the saga’s political and emotional tension.

Book 3

The Orange Light of an Alien Sun

Wonder and New Beginnings

Beyond the Solar System, Callisto and the crew of Shambhala arrive in the Amaterasu system and enter orbit around Bachué, a world filled with beauty, promise, and unanswered questions. What opens before them is not simply a planet to explore, but the daunting and exhilarating possibility of beginning again.

Yet even in the wonder of discovery, absence remains. Separated from Pavani, marked by Naomi’s absence, and far from everything she left behind, Callisto must learn to inhabit a future as uncertain as it is extraordinary. As science becomes a tool for survival, creation, and hope, it also becomes the language through which these women attempt to build a new life.

Emotional core: wonder, loss, creation, and belonging.

Narrative scale: the saga opens onto another world without losing its human intimacy.

Book 4

Infinite Horizons

Reunion and Resolve

When Callisto returns to the Solar System on a covert mission, her reunion with Pavani comes not in a moment of peace, but in the midst of danger. The persecution of the Foundation has deepened, the Moon is under growing military control, and what begins as a carefully planned operation soon becomes a fight to stay one step ahead.

At last together, Callisto and Pavani must navigate a world of surveillance, shifting loyalties, and uncertain refuge, holding fast to one another as the future opens before them in unexpected ways. In the midst of danger, they find not only love, but a new sense of clarity, maturity, and the freedom to choose the life they want to build.

Emotional core: reunion, resolve, maturity, and choice.

Narrative scale: a new horizon for the saga, where intimacy and possibility widen together.
